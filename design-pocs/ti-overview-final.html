Overview — final. Four takeaways the app can work out on its own (your stack against KEV, news indicators already in your evidence, how well your rules cover the actors named, the thinnest-covered technique), the three boxes, and the stories with when they were published, which cases they touch and where they came from. Click an actor or a story for its panel; the panel keeps only the source’s short summary, plus “Open original” when the story has a link (the offline sample stories have none). All data comes from OSINTrix run on its sample data (stories, case matches, the KEV catalogue shipped with the app, MITRE ATT&CK names, your 405 rules); the four-product stack list is the only example input. EPSS and CVSS are not shown because the app only has them after a lookup you switch on.
