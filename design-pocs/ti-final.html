How to read this. Every number, name, date and badge was produced by running OSINTrix itself on its sample data on — the stories, the indicators in your cases, the KEV catalogue that ships with the app, MITRE ATT&CK names and your 405 detection rules. Your stack is the one example thing: four products typed into the stack list. Press Show where each part comes from to see which app function supplies each block, and which blocks are new (a new way of showing data the app already has). Click any story for its panel.
  • No relevance score: the order is explainable — your evidence first, then known-exploited flaws in your stack, then the rest by date.
  • No EPSS or CVSS numbers: the app only has them after a lookup you switch on (FIRST.org, NVD), so the table offers that lookup instead of showing a number it does not have.
  • Times are the stories’ real dates. The sample stories are old, so they show as weeks or years ago — that is what you would see.
